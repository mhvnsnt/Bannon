# The Bastard — God Within Mode Integration

## Status
Concept captured for future implementation. Do not treat this as a current production requirement for the main game build.

## Core idea
After The Bastard has enough completed standalone episodes to establish its identity (target: five full episodes), the series can become a playable narrative addition inside Bannon.

The Bastard is not New Game+ and is not merely a conventional DLC. It is a **secondary selectable playthrough/mode attached to God Within**.

## Player-facing structure
Within the God Within selection area, expose two sibling choices:

- **God Within** — the primary God Within playthrough.
- **The Bastard** — a direct entry into the standalone Bastard experience.

Selecting The Bastard should let the player enter that experience directly, without first completing God Within.

## Narrative relationship
The two experiences remain independently playable, but they can converge. God Within can naturally lead into The Bastard through discoveries, encounters, environmental evidence, or other narrative progression. The Bastard can simultaneously stand on its own as a complete sequence of episodes.

```text
                 GOD WITHIN
                     │
                     │ discoveries / progression / narrative bridges
                     ▼
               THE BASTARD
                     │
                     │ standalone episodes / playable sequences
                     ▼
              MAIN BANNON STORY
```

The relationship should feel like an integrated narrative expansion rather than a detached side mode: the player can enter The Bastard directly, while the main God Within experience can organically uncover its connection.

## Media integration
Once the Bastard series has sufficient finished material, completed episodes can be embedded directly into the game as authored media and/or reconstructed in-engine sequences:

- full-episode playback
- cinematic cutscenes
- chapter/interstitial sequences
- discovered recordings/films
- environmental or diegetic playback
- transitions between filmed Bastard material and gameplay
- eventual playable reconstructions where appropriate

The literal show should be allowed to exist **inside the game**, not merely be referenced by it.

## Production prerequisite
Do not build the full integration prematurely. The five-episode threshold is a creative milestone: enough completed Bastard material must exist to establish the show's visual language, mythology, rhythm, continuity, and recurring physical storytelling before it becomes a substantial game mode.

The current TRIPPEDD pilot may introduce Bannon/The Bastard only through its locked terminal tag. The standalone Bastard series comes later and supplies the deeper body of material for this integration.

## Design principle
**The show becomes a piece of the game world. The game becomes another way to experience the show.**

## Future implementation notes
