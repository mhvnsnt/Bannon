They use the same joint names as the adventurers, so the same clip contract applies.
Soldier_Male, Soldier_Female, Zombie_Male, and Zombie_Female in public/models/humanoid are CC0 full-size characters from Quaternius, about 1.8m.
They use Hips, Abdomen, Torso, UpperArmL, FistL. That skeleton is separate from KayKit and from the Mixamo mannequin.
Full-size bodies are scaled to 1.92m. KayKit stays at 1.5m. Limb bones are not scaled, because that was tearing the skin.
src/game3d/rig-pipeline.ts is the contract: which meshes stay, which clip fills which slot, and that root position is the only track removed.
